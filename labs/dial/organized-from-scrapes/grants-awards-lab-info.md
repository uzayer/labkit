# DIAL grants, awards, and lab-information candidates from public captures

## Scope and handling

This is a schema-oriented working inventory, not an import file or approved
copy. It uses only the 2026-09-12 capture in `labs/dial/scraped-data/` plus the
unchanged `DIAL Proposal.md`, and does not change those files. `Grants` means funding made to the Lab or a project; `Awards`
means recognition. A label that says _grant_, _fund_, _funding_, or contains a
monetary amount is kept out of `Awards`, even when a personal page places it
under an “Awards” heading.

Dates and amounts below are preserved as written. A year or year range is not
silently converted to a Payload `date`, and “lac”/“lakh” is not converted to a
number. No `status`, `featured`, Research Theme, Project, Publication, Media,
Team Member, or identifier relationship is inferred.
