# DIAL frontend and editorial principles

These principles preserve the useful reasoning from the earlier design notes without keeping the block-shopping history.

## Credibility must be concrete

Research labs in the Global South are often asked to prove legitimacy before they are allowed the speculative register available to already-famous institutions. DIAL's site should therefore establish credibility through evidence:

- real Publications and Venues;
- accurately scoped Awards;
- named Projects and the communities involved;
- real Team Members and alumni trajectories;
- collaborators and funders shown in context;
- fieldwork, Lab life, and conference imagery that belongs to DIAL.

Do not compensate with inflated statistics, borrowed prestige, or generic “cutting-edge innovation” language.

## Local identity is not decoration

Bengali Project names, work conducted across Bangladesh, and the constraints of language, bandwidth, devices, safety, and trust are part of the research identity. Present them as substantive context, not exotic visual texture.

Project names should preserve Bengali where the Lab supplies it. Images need accurate captions and consent; placeholder stock photography cannot imply a real DIAL event or community.

## The site has two jobs

For prospective students, it should answer: Is this Lab credible, what would I work on, who would I learn from, and how do I apply?

For peer researchers, collaborators, and funders, it should answer: What does DIAL study, what has it produced, who is involved, and how can I cite or contact it?

The homepage routes visitors. Research and detail pages provide depth. Publications provide academic utility. People and alumni make the mentorship story legible. Join Us keeps recruitment direct.

## Structured evidence beats repeated prose

- Publication, Project, Team Member, Grant, Award, Venue, and Research Theme facts come from Payload.
- Counts are derived from those records.
- Prose that changes rarely may stay hardcoded in the frontend.
- The same Publication and Project components should render consistently wherever they appear.

## Restraint

- Every section must either establish trust or move a visitor toward a useful next step.
- Do not add a feature because a shadcn block exists.
- Treat global search, analytics experiments, elaborate structured data, and new collections as deferred unless they directly move the DIAL launch.
- Prefer a complete, accurate route over a broader but fictional site.
