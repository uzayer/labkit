# DIAL source captures

These files preserve material captured from DIAL's current public site, Dr. Nova Ahmed's personal site, and a DIAL proposal. They are evidence and research inputs, not approved public copy.

## Sources

- `nsu-hci-current-website/` — eight pages from the current NSU HCI/DIAL site, captured 2026-09-12
- `nova-ahmed-personal-site/` — 19 pages from the PI's personal site (including the `/home` alias), captured 2026-09-12
- `DIAL Proposal.md` — a proposal containing Lab background, people, Projects, Publications, and plans

See [`CRAWL-2026-09-12.md`](./CRAWL-2026-09-12.md) for crawl coverage.

The source set is intentionally messy. Pages may be stale, disagree with one another, mix personal and Lab records, or omit canonical URLs.

## How to use it

- Use captures to find plausible Team Members, Projects, Grants, Awards, Publications, collaborators, and wording.
- Cross-check overlapping claims across captures and bibliographic sources.
- Treat a single-source claim as unverified until DIAL confirms it or a suitable primary record supports it.
- Preserve the raw capture even after a normalized record is corrected; record the correction in the curated layer.
- Do not edit raw captures to make them agree with the current model.

The launch database was built from these captures; the intermediate `organized-from-scrapes/` layer was deleted afterwards (recover it from commit `aa9b7c2^`).
