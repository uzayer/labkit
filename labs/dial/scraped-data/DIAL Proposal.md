# `Mission` {#mission}

`Ensure inclusivity and access for marginal ones using minimalist and explainable technology.`

# `Vision` {#vision}

- `Ensure making a positive impact for people, especially marginal communities, using affordable and trustworthy technology solutions and ideation`
- `Scholarly Publication in high-ranked journals and conferences`
- `Present the work in reputed venues to increase visibility`
- `Organize workshops and conferences to disseminate knowledge`
- `Create World-Class Research leadership`

- `The intersection of HCI and AI`
- `XAI`
- `Explainable ML`
