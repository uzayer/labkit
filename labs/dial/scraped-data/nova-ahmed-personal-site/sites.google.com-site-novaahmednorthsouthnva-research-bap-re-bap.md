# Bap re Bap

![working on a wearable](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/sitesv/APaQ0SQmC3ANjB7JwtnNtruz0zm_laJZCYHF9Mm0CK5jVREsOmZUisA1Xs7g9J-nj1VojF6tKONJS9A85m3debhZhy4TmpKOQzzZSfNXSwMhPq_3amKFpHr-kYOfgYVMpUGAvRGgxKjEz8Q5tv12TNWtrAi30__gZnueO5Kf_CpbTA9xIzFSIbhNrqpUnqAUAm0iwpm1THCcrCQKUhNatuW_Zx1iof9KYQ3m33ybU4Q=w1280)

Bap re bap in use

It is a project to look at the stress level of drivers where the traffic situation is difficult in Bangladesh.

It is a low cost wearable that is locally developed and not very good looking.

We received initial seed fund from North South University. Mr. Mirza Md. Lutfe Elahi is the PI of the funding.
