entries:

  - title: Accessibility & Inclusion
    slug: accessibility-inclusion
    shortDescription: >
      Designing technology for marginalised communities — children with autism,
      visually impaired users, elderly populations, low-literacy users, and
      people in resource-constrained settings. Emphasis on locally appropriate
      and low-cost solutions.
    featured: true

  - title: Gender & Feminist HCI
    slug: gender-feminist-hci
    shortDescription: >
      Feminist approaches to human-computer interaction research and design.
      Covers women in computing and STEM, gender-based digital abuse, online
      safety for women, and inclusive workplace environments. Encompasses the
      Aparajita and Protibadi project clusters.
    featured: true

  - title: Mental Health & Wellbeing
    slug: mental-health-wellbeing
    shortDescription: >
      Technology-supported approaches to mental health detection and
      intervention, with focus on low-resource settings. Includes depression
      detection via smartphone behaviour, emotional support systems, and
      wellbeing monitoring for specific populations (drivers, doctors, students).
    featured: true

  - title: Explainable AI & Machine Learning
    slug: explainable-ai-ml
    shortDescription: >
      Research on making AI and machine learning systems interpretable and
      trustworthy, especially in the Bangladesh/Global South context. Includes
      deepfake detection, depression prediction, and XAI frameworks for
      local deployment.
    featured: true

  - title: ICT for Development (ICTD)
    slug: ictd
    shortDescription: >
      Using information and communication technologies to address development
      challenges in Bangladesh and the Global South. Includes fintech adoption,
      technology for garment workers, digital financial services for women,
      and COVID-era technology adoption among marginalised groups.
    featured: true

  - title: Safety & Security
    slug: safety-security
    shortDescription: >
      Technology design for personal safety and digital security, with
      particular focus on women. Includes wearable safety devices, privacy
      research, anti-harassment platforms, and online safety in South Asia.
    featured: false

  - title: IoT & Low-cost Hardware
    slug: iot-low-cost-hardware
    shortDescription: >
      Development of affordable IoT and hardware systems for developing-country
      contexts. Includes the Shonabondhu flood-detection sensor network,
      low-cost wearables for driver stress and rehabilitation, and minimalist
      interface design for constrained devices.
    featured: false

  - title: Infodemic & Misinformation
    slug: infodemic-misinformation
    shortDescription: >
      Understanding and countering health misinformation and infodemic in
      digital spaces, with focus on Bangladesh and South Asia. Closely related
      to COVID-era risk communication research.
    featured: false

  - title: Computing Education & Community
    slug: computing-education-community
    shortDescription: >
      HCI approaches to computing education, ethics in CS education, and
      community engagement for broadening participation in computing. Includes
      summer/winter schools, workshops, and the BanglaCHI chapter activities.
    featured: false
