# Homebrew Documents

## [Copy heading link](https://sites.google.com/view/lodge-blackman-games/homebrew-documents#h.p_6Zo2HMyzxDHR) Warhammer 40k Roleplay

Dark Heresy First Edition

[Humanity's Shield](https://drive.google.com/file/d/0B2b-KRdbOmU5YU9ubG5hUVAxWTA/view?usp=sharing&resourcekey=0-g2JKJ6i0ZvmS3-5H0su5Rw) \- Supplement focusing on the Imperial Guard, includes new abhuman homeworlds, regimental backgrounds, alternate ranks including Sergeant and Commissar paths, and new gear.

Rogue Trader

[Abhuman Character Guide](https://drive.google.com/file/d/0B2b-KRdbOmU5ak9MYjZGVjBqMTQ/view?usp=sharing&resourcekey=0-wXt0bY9beUjD_WDutzVJLw) \- Rules that provide the option to explore the numerous Abhuman strains of humanity present in the Warhammer 40,000 Universe.

[Eldar Character Guide](https://drive.google.com/file/d/0B2b-KRdbOmU5TGVITEdMOWtGZGc/view?usp=sharing&resourcekey=0-mfXBa13cNhOSEqkjD_Bteg) \- Rules for playing the enigmatic Eldar, either as a crafty outcast or a powerful Seer. Includes new Alternate Ranks for Eldar Explorers, Eldar equipment, and guidelines for creating Eldar starships.

[Gamemaster Screen Additional Tables](https://drive.google.com/file/d/0B2b-KRdbOmU5Zkd6WG5KQVU4SDA/view?usp=sharing&resourcekey=0-lvjoAKoptzsRghW6D1K5rA) \- Additional 4 Screens of Tables that can be used by GMs. New screens include acquisition rules, Endeavour and Rewards rules, XP reward rules, fear rules, and Vehicle Combat critical tables.

[Legends of the Expanse](https://drive.google.com/file/d/0B2b-KRdbOmU5ZUhValhWcHBrTGs/view?usp=sharing&resourcekey=0-vVT64rI9GEe2IpVNQjev_w) \- Rules that expand the existing career paths of the Rogue Trader system to match the experience totals in Dark heresy and Deathwatch by adding in 3 new ranks to each existing Career Path. Includes 3 new Career Paths (including their expanded ranks) and a myriad of new equipment.

[Necron Character Guide](https://drive.google.com/file/d/0B2b-KRdbOmU5Qi1xY1kwTWtjNEU/view?usp=sharing&resourcekey=0-mThMWbNifhj-46kKLZgbow) \- Rules for playing an ancient and mysterious memberof the Necron Horde. Includes new Alternate Ranks for Necron Explorers, Necron equipment, and guides for creating Necron starships.

[Tyranid Character Guide](https://drive.google.com/file/d/0B2b-KRdbOmU5Uk1SaWlEMnI0a1E/view?usp=sharing&resourcekey=0-16a6Jp3Xdr8xM0WZXO3OnQ) \- Rules for playing a Ymgarl Tyranid Genestealer who can develop traits of many different strains of Tyranid. Includes new Alternate Ranks for Tyranid Explorers, Tyranid equipment, and guidelines for creating Tyranid starships.

[Xenos Character Guide](https://drive.google.com/open?id=0B2b-KRdbOmU5NEc0RjkyeDItV2M&authuser=0) \- Rules for playing a multitude of the lesser races of the Warhammer 40,00- Universe. Includes rules for 15 lesser races, including: Brachyura, Caryatid, Demiurg, Hrud, Jokaero, Lacrymole, Loxatl, Nicassar, Rak'Gol, Slann, Sslyth, Stryxis, Tarellian, Thyrrus, and Vespid. Includes new Equipment and rules for creating Rak'Gol and Stryxis starships.

Deathwatch

[The Emperor's Angels](https://drive.google.com/file/d/0B2b-KRdbOmU5c05hTldodnRpRjg/view?usp=sharing&resourcekey=0-IIk3Sge4VSN_ex4ib1X81A) \- Rules for new Advanced Specialties, Centurion Armor, Space Marine Starships, and playing beyond Rank 8.

[The Traitor Legions](https://drive.google.com/file/d/0B2b-KRdbOmU5VUszWlBQWDN1enM/view?usp=sharing&resourcekey=0-c1z2lUXTMoEyUOIEkfUDuQ) \- Rules for creating members of the nine Traitor Legions, comes with new Squad Modes, Psychic Powers, and Equipment.

[Auto-Calculating Character Sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1hnxD-q1_SRMxa0mC0VKPqGBKlgDZ8l5NYvkS7esDi0c/edit?usp=sharing)\- This Character Sheet will Auto-Calculate multiple character features for Deathwatch Characters, and will also automatically update Squad Mode and Solo Mode abilities as you progress in Experience.
